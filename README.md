# 🍽️ KhanaKhazana

**KhanaKhazana** is a modern restaurant discovery and food ordering app designed to bring delicious food closer to you.  
Browse nearby restaurants, explore menus, order your favorite dishes, and enjoy exclusive offers — all in one place!

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🏠 Restaurant Listing & Menu Browsing
Explore restaurants around you with detailed menus, ratings, photos, and pricing.

### 💳 Online Payments & Order Tracking
Seamlessly pay online through secure gateways and track your orders in real time.

### ⭐ Reviews & Ratings
Rate your dining experiences and read honest reviews from other foodies.

### 🔍 Cuisine & Dietary Filters
Filter restaurants and dishes by cuisine type or dietary preferences — including **Vegan**, **Jain**, **Gluten-Free**, and more.

### 🔄 Order Again / Favorite Dishes
Reorder your favorite meals with a single tap or save dishes for future cravings.

### 🎁 Loyalty Points & Rewards System
Earn points for every order and redeem them for exciting rewards and discounts.

### 💸 Offers & Coupons Aggregator
Find the best deals, discounts, and coupon codes from your favorite restaurants.
